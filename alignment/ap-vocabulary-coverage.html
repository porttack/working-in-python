Where this list comes from

There is no official AP CSP glossary — the Course and Exam Description has no glossary appendix of its own (see alignment/glossary-map.md, repo-only, not hosted); its vocabulary lives scattered across Essential Knowledge prose and the Exam Reference Sheet's own naming. This list is compiled, not extracted, and every gloss below is written fresh in our own words — no third-party definition is reproduced.

  • The official 2026 AP CSP Exam Reference Sheet (College Board) — pseudocode instructions, operators, Robot commands.
  • Term names only (not prose) from a community-compiled list at apcsexamprep.com.
  • Term names only (not prose) from Khan Academy's AP CSP vocabulary review page.

Best-effort, not authoritative — there is no ground truth to check against. Treat gap rows as candidates to verify, not confirmed absences.

Symbolic, not literal — a Venn diagram plotting all 325 words individually would be illegible. Circle sizes and overlap are illustrative, not area-proportional.
